intro: "This is one of those recipes that contributed to setting up this blog. My ex fell in love with me over this meal and my past flatmates still beg me to make this whenever we reunite.  It is a lot of work, and pretty difficult to keep everything hot at plating time, but well worth the effort. Authentic bibimbap clay pots would probably make this easier. However if you want to impress some friends, give it a try! Some steps can be made ahead " 
ingredients:
  - 1 thick big carrot
  - 1 small turnip, optional or sub more carrot
  - 1 cucumber, peeled 
  - 100 ml rice vinegar
  - 6-8 dried shiitake mushrooms, soaked *
  - 1 thumb ginger
  - 4 cloves garlic
  - 1 spring onion
  - 4-6 tbsp gochujang
  - sesame seeds
  - 2 tsp sesame oil
  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
  - 2 tsp rice vinegar
  - 1 tsp honey/sugar
  - 1 bag spinach
  - 1 block (super) firm tofu, drained from excess liquid
  - 4 eggs
  - 300 g sushi rice
notes: 
  \* Fresh shiitake are possible too, however I find these less tasty than the dried ones.
---

Mushrooms (preferably at least 2 hours in advance)
Start by soaking the shiitake mushrooms in boiling water, covering with something heavy such that theyre submerged.

Carrot pickle (preferably at least 2 hours in advance)  
Start by making the carrot pickle. Peel the carrot and cut it into 1 mm thin slices. Lay them out like toppled dominoes. Press down on the dominoes with your left hand and use your right hand to cut thin matchsticks of carrot. Do the same with the turnip if using. In a small cup, mix a teaspoon of salt and sugar and fill it halfway with hot water. Top up the other half with rice vinegar and pour over the carrots. Let it sit for as long as possible; it then keeps for a few days in the fridge.

Sesame seeds  
Toast a tablespoon in a dry pan.

Chopping  
Peel 4 cloves of garlic. Finely dice two of them and grate the third.  
Peel a piece of ginger of about 3 by 3 cm. Dice half of it and grate the rest. 
Use scissors to cut the stem of the mushrooms and slice them into strips.  
Place the tofu on the side and slice the block into 2 bricks. Place each brick vertically and slice the brick diagonally all the way through, not separating the pieces. Flip horizontally and cut slices, leaving pyramid shape slices. If this is too hard you can also just slice it however you want. 
Cut the cucumber lengthwise, scrape out the seeds with a spoon and cut diagonal slices, add on top of the pickle.

Bibimbap sauce  
Mix 4-6 tablespoons of gochujang with the grated ginger and garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar honey and half of the toasted sesame seeds. Thin with a few tablespoons of water to a sriracha like consistency, and taste.

Tofu  (1 hour in advance)
Place the tofu on the side and slice the block into 2 bricks. Place each brick vertically and slice the brick diagonally all the way through, not separating the pieces. Flip horizontally and cut slices, leaving pyramid shape slices. If this is too hard you can also just slice it into squares.
Arrange the tofu pieces on a baking tray lined with baking paper and pat dry as much excess water as possible. Coat the tofu loosely with 2 tablespoons of the bibimbap sauce loosened with soy sauce and extra honey. Add pepper and bake it on baking paper until somewhat dried out and crispy, approximately 20-30 minutes on 220C.  After the tofu is ready transfer to a ceramic bowl and lower the heat of the oven to 90 C. 

Spinach and Mushrooms
While the tofu is in the oven, you can cook the mushrooms. For this, heat sesame oil in the pan with half some of the cubed garlic and ginger, salt and fry, add mushrooms and cook for a couple minutes till done. If they get very dry you can add soy sauce. Transfer the mushrooms into a ceramic bowl and set aside. In the same pan repeat this step, with the additon of spring onions, for the spinach until its slightly wilted, do NOT add salt to the spinach! Also transfer the spinach to a ceramic bowl.  
Keep the bowls of prepped elements hot in the oven at 90 C. 

Rice
Cook the rice according to the instructions, this will probably take about 20 minutes in total. This is very important, please dont overcook! Season with some salt and just before draining, add a small splash of rice vinegar and 2 tsp sugar. Once the rice is cooking you can add any ceramic or heatproof bowls that you will use for serving into the now cooled down oven.

Eggs 
While the rice cooks, add a layer of sunflower oil with a splash of sesame oil to a frying pan and the fry the eggs softly, the yolk should still be a little runny. Add salt and pepper

Finally assemble the bowls; Start with rice, add all toppings and a fried egg. Over exaggerate the amount of bibimbap sauce (if you like spicy) and finally sprinkle over some sesame seeds.
