Put a pan of water on for the gnocchi and add plenty of salt. Toast a few crumbled walnuts in a dry pan, making sure they don't burn, and put them on a plate as soon as they're done. Very finely dice a shallot, cut the radicchio into strips and fry in olive oil for a few minutes. Deglaze with wine, let it evaporate and then fry the radicchio for a few more minutes. Take the radicchio and onions out of the pan and then, over low heat, melt pieces of gorgonzola in a thin layer of milk or cream in the same pan; optionally add a little grated parmesan. Meanwhile, cook the gnocchi; they need 2 minutes from the moment the water is really boiling. Check whether the gnocchi are done, and if they're still too soft and mushy, cook them longer! Peel the pear and cut it into small cubes. Drain, mix everything together and garnish with the pear cubes and walnuts.
