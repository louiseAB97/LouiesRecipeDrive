In a large pan, put the brown lentils on to cook with 3 bay leaves, about 10 allspice berries and a whole peeled onion. Simmer over low heat until the lentils are soft enough (about an hour? check the packet?). In a separate bowl, mix the sour cream with 2–4 tablespoons of flour into a smooth paste. Once the lentils are soft, stir the flour paste into the soup. At first it looks like it's curdling; that's fine, just keep going. Then add 3 tablespoons of vinegar and two tablespoons of sugar, plus plenty of salt and pepper. Now taste the soup and keep adjusting until the sweet-sour-salty balance is right.  
Note: if you're making a lot of soup, two tubs of sour cream are recommended.  
Tip: lentils triple in volume, so I think about 300 grams of lentils is the maximum for your "biggest pan"? You'd have to test it.
