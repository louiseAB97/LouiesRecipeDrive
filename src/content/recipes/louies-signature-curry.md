Put water on for the rice with a pinch of salt.  
Cut the aubergine to the size you like. If you have enough time: salt it, squeeze it out and fry in (coconut) oil, then set aside. Cut the red onion into thin wedges and chop the ginger. Fry in oil, add about 2 tablespoons of curry paste and fry along. Add the chopped vegetables to the pan (if the aubergine wasn't fried first, it can go in raw now). Straight away add most of the tin of coconut milk too (keep a little aside), because the vegetables will cook in the coconut milk. Add a little water as well so there's enough liquid in the pan. Then add 2 to 3 lime leaves (freezer) and a bruised lemongrass stalk.  
Now put the rice in the boiling water, turn the heat down and set a timer for 10 minutes. When the timer goes off, drain the rice and leave it to steam with the lid on until all the vegetables in the curry are cooked. For flavour, add a splash of fish sauce and a little sugar. Add the chickpeas in the last 2 min. Serve with rice and coriander or Thai basil.
