Put on a pan of generously salted water. Taste it if you like; it should be as salty as the sea.  
Slice a white onion into half-rings, fry in olive oil over low heat until lightly caramelised, deglaze with wine and add the tomato sauce.  

Let the sauce bubble away until the pasta is done, and meanwhile slice 4 balls of mozzarella. In the meantime, cook the pasta (2 min less than stated on the packet).  

Put everything in an oven dish in two layers and sprinkle with basil and parmesan.  
Bake for 20 min in the oven at 200 degrees.
