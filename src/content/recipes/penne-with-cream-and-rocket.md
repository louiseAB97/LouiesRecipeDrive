Put water on with salt and meanwhile thinly slice about 4 to 5 cloves of garlic. Because the sauce is very quick, only start making it once the pasta is in the water. Heat a generous tablespoon of olive oil (optionally 50/50 with butter) in a frying pan and fry the garlic until golden. At that point, deglaze with a splash of wine and turn the heat down. Once the wine has reduced a little, add the cream plus salt and pepper. When the pasta has about 2 minutes of cooking time left, stir the bag of rocket into the cream. Add the pasta and garnish with parmesan cheese.
