Peel the squash and cut it lengthwise into half-centimetre slices. Put olive oil in a baking tray with the squash, salt, sugar and thyme; halfway through, add 2 shallots cut into wedges. Meanwhile, make the béchamel. Medium heat > cook the butter and flour for 3 minutes. Add almost all the milk while stirring, and thin with a little more later if needed. Season the béchamel with salt, pepper and nutmeg. Then fry the spinach with 1 chopped shallot in olive oil. Layering: first 1 thin layer of béchamel. Then lasagne sheets. Then squash with pieces of cheese and spinach. Then pasta again, a few dollops of béchamel, squash, cheese, spinach. Top layer: only béchamel on the pasta, and parmesan. Tasty: a little red wine around the edges towards the end of the baking time. 45 min at 230 degrees.
