ingredients:
  - "½ pack of bucatini"
  - "1 tin of sardines in oil"
  - "1 white onion"
  - "2 tablespoons tomato paste (easiest is to just keep a tube in the fridge)"
  - Breadcrumbs
  - Raisins
notes: |
  \* First halve the onion lengthwise, then make 3 or 4 lengthwise cuts and one cut parallel to the chopping board, then cut crosswise many times so you get very small brick-shaped pieces.
---

Bring the pasta water to the boil. Start by toasting the breadcrumbs in a frying pan with a little olive oil and salt, then set aside. Finely dice the onion* and gently fry it in olive oil until slightly caramelised, about 3 min. Meanwhile, open the tin of sardines and decide whether they're big enough that you want to remove the backbone. Once the onion looks good, add the sardines, the tomato paste and the raisins. Turn the heat right down and stir the sauce now and then until the pasta is cooked. If it threatens to stick, add some pasta water to the sauce, and do that at the end anyway so it isn't too dry.  
Drain the pasta and stir it through the sauce while the pan is still on a low heat.
