For the batter, mix chickpea flour (about 120 g per person) with water to a batter consistency. Let it rest for half an hour or longer. Fry the pancakes in olive oil in a medium-hot frying pan; if the batter doesn't spread nicely, you can use the spatula to spread it out a little. Let it cook for a bit, and once it comes loose you can carefully flip it. Cut the vegetables into strips and serve with the hummus.
