Start by cooking the rice, and don't forget the salt. This dish came about by accident when I ran out of Thai curry paste. Cut the aubergine into quarter rings 1 cm thick. Roughly chop the ginger and garlic, and cut the onion into wedges suitable for stir-frying. Heat some neutral olive oil and first fry the aubergine until it's a little charred. Then add some of the oil from the top of the curry paste jar and fry the garlic, ginger and onion in it. Then add 3 tablespoons of the curry paste and fry for about 2 more min. Meanwhile, cut the tofu into fairly large bite-size cubes and add them. Let it fry for a bit, and after not too long, put a lid on so it cooks faster. After a few minutes, once the tofu and aubergine are cooked, add the amount of coconut milk you like, about 150–200 ml, and let it simmer over high heat for a little while so the sauce caramelises a bit without burning. Serve with rice, chilli and coriander.
