Mix everything together and check whether the acidity is right. Not serving it straight away? Then taste again just before serving to check it's still sharp enough.

Be creative. Everything tastes good with chimichurri.  
Tried with: prawns, tofu, sweet potato, bulgur, tomatoes, sandwiches with hummus and chimichurri; it even works as a salad dressing.
