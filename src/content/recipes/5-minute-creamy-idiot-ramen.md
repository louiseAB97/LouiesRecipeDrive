ingredients:
  - 1 pack of precooked (ramen) noodles * 
  - 1 cube cm of fresh ginger, finely diced
  - 1 tbsp of (green) curry paste **
  - 1 tsp of oil
  - 100 ml coconut milk
  - 2 cremini mushrooms, sliced
  - 200 g firm tofu, cubed
  - half a stock cube of choice (veggie/beef/chicken)
  - 250 ml water
  - 2 lime leaves (optional)
  - handfull of spinach
  - fresh coriander 
  - 1 spring onion 
  - (kashmiri) chili flakes
notes: |
  \* Vacuum pack, or frozen noodles of choice if you have those in your freezer, as long as they are precooked so that they take max 1 min to cook. Frozen are actually the tastiest noodles in my opinion but they are a little harder to get by.

  \*\* I highly recommend getting a good quality authentic curry paste from the asian supermarket. They come as buckets with a big vacuum bag of curry paste inside of it. Lasts for ages in the fridge. I used green curry paste but yellow would work too, in that case omit the curry powder.
---

Start by cooking some water in a watercooker. Meanwhile cube the tofu and cut the ginger and mushrooms. In a separate corner of the chopping board slice a spring onion and pick the coriander leaves.

Heat oil in a sauce pan and add the ginger and mushrooms. Fry for a minute on medium heat and add the currypaste and stock cube. Cook for another 20 seconds and add the coconut milk, hot water, curry powder and lime leaves if using. 

Once the mixture boils add in the tofu cubes, and the spinach. On top of that place your noodles and cook for 1 minute. Make sure not too cook much longer than that, to prevent the noodles from turning soft.    

Taste, adjust salt and and pour into your most beautiful bowl. Top with spring onions, coriander and kashmiri chili powder.
