The nice thing about this meal is that everything can be done in 1 pan. Put water on with salt and check the cooking time of the pasta. Plan when the rest needs to go in based on that. The frozen sprouts go in with the pasta about 5 minutes before the end of the cooking time, and the halved cherry tomatoes about 2 min before the end. While the pasta is cooking, you can cut the sun-dried tomatoes into strips and set the table. When everything is done cooking, drain the water. Then stir in a few tablespoons of pesto and the remaining ingredients. If your basil plant is still alive, it's also nice to add some of that. Stir the salad through at the end.
