Start by cutting the steak into strips, patting it dry with kitchen paper and then loosely sprinkling cornflour over it. Heat a pan with sunflower oil and let it get really hot. Fry the steak strips for about 1 minute without stirring too much; it has to sear! Take them out of the pan and set aside. Fry the vegetables in the same pan and meanwhile cook the noodles without salt. In the meantime, make a sauce of soy sauce, honey, grated ginger, grated garlic, a tiny bit of cornflour and plenty of black pepper. Add the noodles and the sliced chilli to the vegetables and add about 1 large tablespoon of sesame oil. Let the noodles sear a little, then add the sauce and the steak. If it's too dry, add a little more sesame oil.
