Wash and dry the peppers, and remove the stems by carefully breaking them off (don't cut them off, or they literally become little cups that fill up with oil). Fry the peppers in batches in a layer of sunflower oil; slightly charred with a puffed-up skin is good. Drain on kitchen paper. If there's still oil left in the pan, pour it out or wipe it away with kitchen paper. Heat the olive oil and fry the whole garlic cloves in it until slightly golden, then add the tomatoes and crush them a bit with a wooden spoon. Let the sauce simmer, as you would any tomato sauce, long enough for it to caramelise a little. Also roughly add basil to the sauce. Then let the peppers simmer in it for about 5 more min. Add the pasta and top with cheese.
