Finely dice the red onion, the pepper too, and chop the parsley and jalapeños. Mix with the drained tuna, a tablespoon of mayo and a teaspoon of ketchup. Add plenty of black pepper.  
Cut the rolls open and top them with the tuna mixture and thin half-slices of tomato, then put the cheese on top. Bake in the oven until done. Serve with lettuce and a 2:1 mayo:ketchup sauce.
