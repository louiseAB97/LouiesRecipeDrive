ingredients:
  - 90 g sugar (from here on for the pastry cream)
  - 1 sachet of vanilla sugar or vanilla essence
  - 250 ml milk
  - 2 egg yolks
  - 45 g flour
  - Pinch of salt
  - 250 ml whipping cream
  - 2 capfuls of whiskey or Strega liqueur
  - 100 g flour (from here on for the choux buns)
  - 75 ml milk
  - 75 ml water
  - 62 g butter
  - 2 eggs
  - 250 ml milk (from here on for the glaze)
  - Small splash of cream
  - 1 small tablespoon of flour
  - 1 heaped tablespoon of cocoa, or more
  - Half a bar of dark chocolate, or a little less
  - A few granules of instant coffee
  - 2 tablespoons sugar

Makes 14 medium choux buns.

**Crema pasticcera**

Start with the pastry cream. Whisk the egg yolks with the sugar and add a little milk. Then whisk in the flour and the rest of the milk. Gently bring the mixture to the boil and keep stirring with a whisk until it's slightly thicker than runny custard. Let it cool, then carefully fold it into the whipped cream together with the alcohol.

**Choux buns**

Melt the butter in the milk and water in a pan over medium-high heat.
Once it has melted, add the flour. Keep stirring and cook the mixture for about 3 minutes.  
Then beat in the eggs one at a time. The mixture should be thick enough to pipe upwards, but not so thick that it becomes hard to pipe.  
Once the mixture has cooled a little, put it in a piping bag and pipe choux bun shapes onto baking paper. Bake for 35 min at 180 degrees. DON'T OPEN THE OVEN IN BETWEEN!

Once the buns have cooled, fill them with the pastry cream using the piping bag with a nozzle, and optionally let them firm up a bit in the freezer. Filling the buns is fiddly, so to make it easier you can make the opening fairly big. The first time I used a piping bag without a nozzle, but with a nozzle it's a million times easier. The chocolate glaze can go over them at the last moment.

**Chocolate glaze**

Bring everything except the chocolate to the boil in a pan. Then turn off the heat and add the chocolate. This sauce stays runny and is a bit custard-like, super tasty.
