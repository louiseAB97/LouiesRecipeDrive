Salt the chicken legs all over with quite a lot of salt, and rub them with curry powder and olive oil.  
Per chicken leg, peel 2 to 3 white onions and halve them lengthwise. You also need about 1 pepper per leg; cut it roughly into about 5 pieces. Mix the onions and peppers well with olive oil, put them in an oven dish or on a baking tray together with the chicken legs and bake at 220 degrees until cooked. While baking, turn the legs now and then and baste the vegetables well with the juices from the chicken. When the chicken is almost done, cook some rice.
