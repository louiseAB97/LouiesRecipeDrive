Start with the roasted chickpeas and the cashews: turn the oven on to 200 °C. Take the chickpeas out of the tin, pat them dry with kitchen paper and mix with 2 tablespoons of sunflower oil, then add salt, 1 tbsp garam masala, ½ tsp smoked paprika powder, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp ground ginger and pepper; roast for 20 min or so. Optionally add small tomatoes with a little olive oil in a separate oven dish. Put the cashews in an oven dish and add them to the oven halfway through the roasting time; we want them slightly browned, so make sure they don't burn. Then cook the pearl couscous in a pan of salted water, and cook the eggs in the same pan at the same time (for soft-boiled they need 5 min). Drain the couscous and immediately add one or two peeled garlic cloves, then pour a little neutral olive oil over it (the heat of the couscous makes the garlic flavour infuse into the oil). Put the lid back on the pan. Then cut the vegetables and boil/microwave the broccoli florets. Briefly toast 2 tablespoons of cumin seeds in a dry frying pan until fragrant. Build the bowls by adding the couscous first, then all the vegetables, then the halved egg and a big tablespoon of beetroot hummus. Finish with some toasted cumin seeds. Optionally sprinkle a little mint and lemon over it.
