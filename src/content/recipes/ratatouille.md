Heat a stainless steel pan until it's so hot that drops of water roll around on it. Meanwhile, quarter the aubergine lengthwise and cut it into generous 1 cm slices. Put 3 tablespoons of neutral olive oil in the pan and straight away the aubergine and salt. Turn the heat down and let the aubergine brown a little, then turn it with a spatula. In the meantime, chop the garlic very roughly and cut the onion into 6 wedges and then in half, so you have big pieces. Push the aubergine to one side of the pan and add the extra virgin oil, the garlic and the onion. Then add the courgette, also in quarter slices, the pepper in pieces of similar size, the mushrooms in quarters and the thyme. Once everything has fried for a bit, the tomatoes can go in, also roughly chopped, along with a few sprigs of parsley. Then put the lid on the pan and meanwhile cook the brown rice. Towards the end, add the crumbled piece of stock cube to the ratatouille as well, plus half a small glass of water. It's done when everything is nicely caramelised.
