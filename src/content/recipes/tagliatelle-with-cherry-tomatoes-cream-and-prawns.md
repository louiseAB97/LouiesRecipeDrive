This meal is a little less simple, but perfectly doable and super tasty.  
Put water on with salt and fry half a bag of prawns in a tablespoon of olive oil in a frying pan. Quite a bit of liquid will probably come out; if it's really a lot you can pour it off, but you can also leave it and stir it through the sauce as a kind of prawn stock. Meanwhile, cut 4 to 5 cloves of garlic into thick slices and halve the cherry tomatoes. If you're using fresh chilli, slice it into thin strips now as well. By now your prawns are probably reasonably warm (they don't need to be fully cooked yet). Move the prawns (including the liquid) to a bowl and wipe the pan clean. Now put the pasta on. Now we make the sauce: add olive oil again (mixed with butter if possible) to the pan and fry the garlic in it. When the garlic turns slightly golden, add the tomatoes too. Let them fry for a few minutes until they almost catch, then deglaze with wine and turn the heat down. Now add the prawns back in as well (optionally with the liquid). Once it has all reduced a bit (after about 2 minutes), add the cream and the chilli, plus salt and pepper. Let the sauce simmer until the pasta is ready. Taste the sauce along the way to check whether it's salty and sharp enough (otherwise add some extra wine and salt). Add the pasta to the sauce and garnish with chopped parsley. TIP: if it's all too dry, you can loosen the sauce with milk (you can even do this afterwards, or when reheating leftovers).
