  \* Notes on curry powder: every country has its own blend. I tried to recreate the Japanese blend: https://www.okonomikitchen.com/japanese-curry-powder/ — look at the ingredient list for inspiration, make sure it at least contains ginger, and otherwise add it yourself.
  I had hot Madras curry from the Indian shop and added a pinch of cinnamon, cloves, grated ginger, ground coriander and extra turmeric, and that was perfect (cardamom was the only thing still missing, but it isn't necessary).

Cook the rice. Meanwhile, roughly chop the carrot, onion and garlic and fry in a frying pan until cooked, then add the curry powder and optionally a little (fresh or ground) ginger, salt and pepper. Fry for 2 more min and then add the coconut milk. Peel the aubergine and cut it into slices of about 1.5 cm, but no thicker than that. Mix flour and water with some salt and pepper to the consistency of pancake batter and put the panko in a separate bowl. Dip the aubergine slices in the batter and then in the panko and deep-fry; sprinkle some coarse salt on them after frying. Blend the sauce with a stick blender and add a little honey or sugar to taste. Reheat the sauce in the frying pan if needed. Put the rice on a plate, spoon the sauce next to it with the aubergine on top and sprinkle with spring onion and sesame.
